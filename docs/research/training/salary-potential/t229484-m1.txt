After going through a couple versions of this on my own and with some suggestions from guys on the USA-Offsite, I think it's ready for the broader community:
Now v2.6.S25!
.xls (https://drive.google.com/file/d/0ByNXe6cWgZCed1FLd2dYWERXW...)
.ods (https://drive.google.com/file/d/0ByNXe6cWgZCedGF3NzlERGZ5R...)
Once you click on this link, open the "file" menu and select "download."
This spreadsheet is a tool for modeling training based on the information gathered from the BB Training Project. It allows you to input skills, heights, ages, potential, trainer level, weeks of training and which trainees get minutes. And it models the training, including the elastic effect. You must download it and open it on your computer for it to work.
Please let me know if you have any questions, problems or suggestions and I'll do my best to respond quickly. Enjoy!
And please help this tool get more accurate and more useful by contributing data to the BB Training Project! Check it out here: (203921.1)
EDIT: v2.6.S25 – Updated for Season 25
EDIT: v2.6.S24 – Updated for Season 24, "SP/$1k" replaces the opaque "Salary Efficiency" metric
EDIT: v2.6.S23 – Updated for Season 23
EDIT: v2.6 – All coefficients updated to include the most recent data from the Training Site (203921.364)
EDIT: v2.5.4 – Display age during/after training button, past dates cross out, empty player slots gray
EDIT: v2.5.3 – Fixed Ssn 24 Training Regimen Drop-down boxes
EDIT: v2.5.2 – New page for improved skill input process, salary efficiency metric, date specific training (allows you to keep past training regimen), Salary Estimator update for Ssn 22, 10 seasons and 12 players.
EDIT: v2.4 – Added the salary estimator from the BB Training Site. Also created better instructions and explanations.
EDIT: v2.3 – Fixed a bug that made 6'11" and 6'10" guys train like they were 6'1"
EDIT: v2.2 – Fixed a bug in how the elastic effect was calculated.
