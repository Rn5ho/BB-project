## 1. Headline: the optimizer would have roughly doubled gate delivery — with two honest caveats

Across the 6 Centri players observed reaching age 21: **reality passed 11/20 owner
viability gates (39 levels of shortfall); the optimizer's prescriptions from the same
draft states, same real staff, pass 18/20 (2 levels short)**. Per player: Koštomaj
0/3→3/3, Jerin 1/3→3/3, Jalovec 3/4→4/4, Ostroveršnik 3/4→4/4; Piršič and Plesavec
stay fail-1 (age-19 start / awkward draft shape).

**Caveat A (verifier/critic):** prescriptions sit at +0/+1 margins, so under the
engine's calibrated ±1 level tolerance the honest prescription range is **14–18/20** —
still clearly above 11/20, and the big misses (IS −8/−9) are 8–10× engine noise.
**Caveat B (critic, the important one):** 18/20 scores {optimizer + big-class filter};
the RAW value-v0 ranking would have sent 206cm pot-9 Plesavec down a wing path (the
cross-class gate-count bias, C1 below). And the 11/20 "reality failed" verdict is
**build-library-contingent**: all three pjtr IS-gate failures produced players
(ID 15-17, RB 13-16, SB 17, IS 5-7) that are shape-wise the Greek **rim protector** —
a build class the bronze medalists field (2 of their 5 centers: IS 7-9, SB 20-21) and
the owner's library *doesn't contain*. Part of "reality failed" may be "the library is
missing a legitimate build pjtr was deliberately building." → owner decision #1.

**Misallocation, not speed, either way:** observed entry TSP beat the prescriptions'
M1 TSP on all six players (+3..+10) while gate value was far worse — the owner's
"high TSP without gates is misallocated" thesis, live. pjtr fed SB to 17 (owner target
8-10) while IS sat at 5-7; alenokc shorted ID by 2-4 spreading into HA/DR/JS.

**Staff sensitivity:** alenokc's clean verdicts are staff-carried — at coach 6/YT 6
(the realistic club ask) Koštomaj and Jerin each lose the last ID level by M1. pjtr
upgrades flip nothing. Binding constraint: staff at the margin for elite-staffed
18yo bigs; draft state for late/awkward profiles.

## 2. The shared-slot constraint is (nearly) free — if you steer same-class prospects together

pjtr's real handicap was NOT that three players share one weekly training: a single
shared 53-week sequence (IS×25, ID×13, RB×10, JS×5) matches all three players'
individual optima simultaneously (gate shortfalls identical; by M2 all three pass
every owner gate). The club's actual sequence (SB×23, ID×11, RB×9, PA×9, **IS×0**)
left ~8-10 gate levels per player on the table vs that same-constraint optimum.
**Cross-class is where the slot fights**: 2 bigs + 1 wing → the wing absorbs
essentially the whole cost (OD 8 vs 13 at entry, −14 TSP). Coexistence matrix:
big↔big overlap 0.75-0.90 (free), outside↔outside 0.46-0.81, cross-block 0.02-0.23.
**Coach-handoff rule: same-class prospects to the same club; never park a lone wing
at a bigs club.** (Also: the shared search found a *better* M1 line for Piršič than
his solo prescription — beam-path slack ≈ 1 level; treat ±1 verdicts as ties.)

  incl. one in-the-wild pot-6-style inside-attacking wing shape (DR17/HA16/IS17/RB5).

## 4. The JR question: answered

**Zero players at JR≥16 in the entire 4,042-player age-20/21 full-skill universe.**
JR≥14: 9 players (0.22%), all ≤201cm, a third of whom sacrificed the OD gate for it.
Greek squad max: 12. Price tag: JR 13→16 on a JS-16 SG costs ~7 weeks + 1.50 SG cap
points — the most cap-expensive +3 at SG shape; the same weeks buy the whole OD 13→16
gate move. **Nobody is running the hypothesized tactic; if it has match value, it is
genuinely virgin territory at a known (steep) price.** Match value stays owner judgment.

## 5. The HA-elastic question (owner batch 2): answered, against the practice

- The od←ha 0.007 elastic that motivates HA 18-19 contributes **0.008-0.024 OD
  sublevels per extra HA week** — 2-7% of what one direct OD week delivers. Negligible.
- The REAL benefit of HA/DR above OD is **top-skill-malus avoidance**, and it
  saturates at OD+1 — **HA 16-17 captures it; the 18-19 push buys nothing mechanical**.
- Bigger surprise: **OD-first sequencing harvests MORE elastic** (ha←od is 0.05,
  7× the reverse pair): +0.6-0.7 total sublevels per 20-week budget vs HA-first.
- Crossover: the marginal 1v1 week beats a direct OD week until HA ~16 (if HA/DR are
  valued to their 15-16 minimums) or until ~19 only if their intrinsic value to 19 is
  real — the elastic never decides it. → owner decision #3 (revise club advice?).

## 6. Guards-vs-wings 1v1 (owner batch 2/3): rule confirmed + refined

From IS~1, guards-1v1 is confirmed: reaches the JS-14 gate a **full season earlier**
(wk 17-18 vs 30-32). Breakevens: over a full 42-week arc, wings overtakes from
starting IS 4/3/1 at 188/196/203cm; over a realistic 20-week block, guards wins
almost everywhere. Two refinements: (a) taller wings flip earlier (height multiplier
on the IS cell); (b) **guards overshoot warning** — 42 guards weeks drive JS to
internal ~21.9 (5+ sublevels wasted past the display cap); plans should switch rows
once JS clears its band.
