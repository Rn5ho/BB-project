## 1. The rates are right in aggregate; the errors are timing and one club factor

**Cumulative volume (timing-free, verified):** bbscout predicts 175.9 internal levels of
growth vs 181 observed displayed levels — **−2.8%**. CP −30.0%, OSL −9.2%. Anchored
(teacher-forced) replay independently reproduces the ratio (0.969).

**But the near-zero total is a cancellation.** The dominant error structure is a
**club-level multiplicative tilt**: bbscout runs ~13% cold on everything at pjtr576
(obs/pred 1.154) and ~22% hot on everything at alenokc (0.780) — a ×1.48 between-club
ratio, stable across segment groups and shared in direction by all three models. Staff
configs were verified against raw weeks.csv — this is not a transcription error.
Club is confounded with training-row mix, coach level (5–6 vs 7) and TC (0 vs 3); the
16 cases cannot separate these. (One weak discriminator: Passing was trained at both
clubs and errs in opposite directions — leaning "club factor" over "row-rate error".)

**Per-skill "cold spots" mostly dissolve into the club tilt.** Raw signed errors put SB
at −4.4 levels; after club rescaling SB is clean (+0.7) — SB was only trained at the
cold club. Surviving within-club residuals beyond ~2σ (all at pjtr576): RB +4.6 and
ID +3.1 (over), OD −3.1 (scatter-only skill: 5 observed pops vs 1.7 EV), IS −2.1
(borderline; trained only via 0.05 secondary cells).

**SB adjudication (two agents, opposite attributions, both "high"):** the anchored ledger
reads SB as the one robust cold skill (19 cold / 4 hot corrections, levels 0.854); the
rate-check reads the same quantum as the club factor wearing SB's clothes (clean after
rescale, while RB/ID at the same club run hot). Both agree SB under-predicts at pjtr576;
**whether it is SB-specific or club-wide is unresolved** — the deciding data (inside rows
trained at the OTHER club) exists in the un-cased alenokc middle era (see §7).

**One genuine anomaly:** Gojc Jalovec (216 cm C, pot 7, age 20) gained ZERO rate-skill
levels in 5 full-minute weeks (3× Passing(PG) + 2× 1v1(SF/PF); verified genuine in raw
cards) vs 3.9 predicted — alone ~30% of alenokc's overprediction. Candidate explanations
split: position-minutes gap (a C getting no PG/SF minutes the cases can't see) vs cap
stage stronger than ×0.725 at his depth (C-weighted sum 22.66 vs stage offsets 22/23/24).
n=1, unresolved.

## 2. Pop timing: the miss ledger fully decomposed (verified)

123 misses = **62 phase-shift ±1wk + 10 ±2wk + 51 genuine**; 72 of 99 FAs are the same
pop at the wrong week. Timing-tolerant recall: **66.3% (±1), 71.8% (±2)**. Matched pairs
directionally balanced (34 early / 38 late) — no systematic rate bias in the timing errors.

Of the 51 genuine misses:
- **22 are scatter-only pops** — the week's training has no rate cell for the skill; the
  gym-EV trickle (0.005–0.016 lvl/wk) structurally cannot pop on schedule. Expected
  misses under an EV model of a random process, not rate errors.
- **29 are rate-trained, concentrated on tiny cells**: observed pops on cells ≤0.2 lvl/wk
  were hit 3/55 (0/30 on ≤0.16); cells ≥0.4 were hit 55/104. A 0.05–0.2 cell has a
  5–20-week pop period, so ±0.5 sublevel start error alone throws timing beyond ±2wk —
  whether those cells are also underestimated is **not decidable** here.

**Pop-count tracks never diverge by more than 1** across all 88 case×skill tracks
(157 predicted vs 181 observed pops total). Cumulative projections are trustworthy to
±1 displayed level per skill per segment on this data.

Anchored-replay honesty split: 57.5% anchored recall = 67 unassisted (37.0%) + 37
"granted" by a prior hot snap; anchored FAs are almost never inventions (only ~5–7 of 90
spurious; 48% are mechanical re-FA chains, the rest timing-adjacent).

## 3. Hidden sublevels dominate week-level error (in-sample bound)

Fitting one static starting sublevel per (case, skill) — 153 parameters, **in-sample** —
lifts recall 32.0% → **72.9%** and cuts FAs 99 → 33; finals MAE 0.300 → 0.213. Best-fit
offsets do not cluster at extremes (consistent with genuinely unknowable sublevels, not a
biased midpoint). Residual misses under fitted starts: 49, dominated by mid-series drift
on heavily-trained skills in the long pjtr576 segments (sb 15, id 7, rb 6). Note the rung
ordering surprised us: fitted starts beat weekly anchoring (57.5%) on recall — anchoring
corrects state but never phase-optimizes. In-sample caveat: 1.18 pops per fitted
parameter; treat 72.9% as an upper bound.

## R2.1 The ×1.48 "club tilt" is NOT a rate difference — it decomposes

**DMI-inversion (verified, quantization-free):** weekly ln-DMI increments on GS9
stable-display pairs isolate real internal skill growth (deflator-free — D never enters
DMI increments). Result: **bbscout's weekly inside-skill rates are essentially exact**
(pooled obs/pred 0.991, n=32/33 pairs; per-training pjtr576: SB-C 0.987, ID-C ~1.02,
RB 1.05). Cross-club ratio-of-ratios **1.10 [0.98–1.20]** — the pop-channel's ×1.48 is
excluded as a rate phenomenon at P < 5e-5 (0 of 20k bootstrap draws). Control: the same
channel shows coach-parrot under-predicting ×1.49, as its −30% volume predicts.
→ Round 1's tilt is **level/pop bookkeeping** (start sublevels — likely draftee low
starts at alenokc — segment endpoints, scatter pops), not a training-speed error.

**Top-skill malus is the prime suspect for the pjtr576 cold side (third-club agent,
MEDIUM):** three independent within-club tests contradict the ×0.925 malus, and a
full-engine rerun with malus OFF eliminates the pjtr cold tilt (1.154→1.029) while
leaving alenokc nearly unchanged (0.780→0.761) and lifting recall 32→38% at equal FA.
BUT malus-off overshoots globally (volume 1.029→0.945 hot, MAE 0.300→0.325) — so the
evidence points at the malus **shape** (0.925^(skill−avg) ≈ ×0.5–0.6 for specialist
centers with top skill 8 levels above average) being too strong at large gaps, not at
removing it. Owner decision; a capped/shallower malus is the candidate family.

**Middle-era windows (verified):** alenokc's never-cased wk7–36 rejects the uniform
club factor — inside PRIMARY cells at the "hot" club run on the COLD side (obs/pred
1.136 yt7; z vs the 0.780 club hypothesis +2.8, vs 1.0 only +1.07). RB/ID at alenokc

## R2.6 Verdict on the owner's hunch ("projections train a bit faster than reality")

**Weekly training rates are not fast** — the quantization-free channel puts them at
−1% (0.991) overall, and the pop channel says totals run 3–4% SLOW. The optimism the
owner feels is real but lives in the **projection assumptions**, not the rates:
full-minutes-every-week (reality: injuries, partial weeks, week-14 squeeze), midpoint
sublevels for draftees (fresh 18yos may start near x.0), cap corners (Jalovec-style
freezes deeper than ×0.725), and EV scatter that smooths lumpy reality. The direct test
is a Neon backtest — project every tracked Slovenian prospect from an old full snapshot
through his club's inferred training + ACTUAL recorded minutes, compare to the realized
next snapshot — queued as round 3.
