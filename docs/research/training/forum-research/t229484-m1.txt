After going through a couple versions of this on my own and with some suggestions from guys on the USA-Offsite, I think it's ready for the broader community:
Now v2.3!
(https://docs.google.com/open?id=0ByNXe6cWgZCeZWtSbkZyUmlkd...)
This spreadsheet is a tool for modeling training based on the information gathered from the BB Training Project. It allows you to input skills, heights, ages, potential, trainer level, weeks of training and which trainees get minutes. And it models the training, including the elastic effect.
Please let me know if you have any questions, problems or suggestions and I'll do my best to respond quickly. Enjoy!
And please help this tool get more accurate and more useful by contributing data to the BB Training Project! Check it out here: (203921.1)
EDIT: v2.3 – Fixed a bug that made 6'11" and 6'10" guys train like they were 6'1"
EDIT: v2.2 – Fixed a bug in how the elastic effect was calculated.
