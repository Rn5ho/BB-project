-- Please post your general questions about training in the help forums (/BBWeb/Forum/read.aspx?folder=64) . This thread is training speed analysis only
-- Have in mind that since this thread was opened, the coaches in BB have changed. An old 9-10 coach is now equal to a level 5 coach, old 7-8 is equal to 3, and so forth. Levels 6 and 7 are new, and they work very slightly better than an old level 10 coach.
This thread is a continuation of Training Speed Analysis (381.1) .
If you have any ideas, post them here or post your team's results here and we'll try to figure out some base ideas for each type.
Last week, I trained Free Throws and 7 out of 19 players popped. While they obviously could be high or low sublevels, based on that alone it would seem to take approximately 2.5 weeks for Free Throws. If anyone else has trained Free Throws, feel free to agree or disagree.
With as many users as there are now, it shouldn't take too long to figure this out.
All Guesses assume a Level 9/10 trainer and 18/19 year old trainee. (GM edit: A level 9/10 coach is a lvl 5 coach now.)
Stamina and Free Throws do NOT depend on trainer level.
