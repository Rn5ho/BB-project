Hello, I am interested in what training plans are you using, what training plans can you offer for every position?
My training plans for every position:

PG ir SG:
1) 1 on 1 PG/SG or SF/PF to driving and dribling will be 13, (it creates elasticity effect for all upper skills)
2) then train pressure to 13,
3) and equally jump range, passing.
4) Then again train 1 on 1 to driving and dribling will be 15 and then again turn around, etc. until potential will reach the limit...

SF:
1) 1 on 1 SF/PF to driving and dribling will be 13, (it creates elasticity effect for all upper skills)
2) inside defense to 13, (it creates elasticity effect for all lower skills + outside defense)
3) then train pressure to 13,
4) inside shot to 13 (it creates elasticity effect for rebounding)
5) and if you are not hurrying, everything else to 13.
6) Then again 1 on 1 SF/PF to driving and dribling will be 13 (or more), then train pressure to 13 (or more), inside defense to 13 (or more), and then depends what kind of player do you want...

PF:
1) inside defense to 13, (it creates elasticity effect for inside shot, rebounding, shot blocking and jump shot)
2) inside shot to 13, (it creates elasticity effect for rebounding and shot blocking)
3) rebounding to 13. (it creates elasticity effect for shot blocking)
4) 1 on 1 SF/PF to driving and dribling will be 13,
5) Then anew depends what kind of player do you want, train something in bonus and then turn around inside shot, defense, rebounding...

C :
1) inside defense to 13, (it creates elasticity effect for inside shot, rebounding, shot blocking and jump shot)
2) inside shot to 13, (it creates elasticity effect for rebounding and shot blocking)
3) rebounding to 13 (it creates elasticity effect for shot blocking)
4) shot blocking to 13.
5) and if you want something of upper skills (jump shot, passing). Then turn around with 15 or 16.

These are only training plans that I suggest, offer yours, criticize mine ;)

___________________________________________________________________

There are 7 (or more) coefficients which includes training:

1. Training type - (295510.11)
2. Age - (295510.6)
3. Height - (295510.5)
4. Elasticity effect - 1 or higher, in shorter words driving and Handling add elasticity effect to all upper skills, inside defense adds elasticity effect to all inside skills. But every skill have skills from which it gains elasticity effect if average of those skills is higher than skill that you train. Sorry for complicated explanation but elasticity effect is complicated thing
5. Cross training (+gym) - ~0.93, 0.07 to the one skill that you don't train (but because of cross training, you will train).
6. Potential - 1.00 if you haven't reached the potential cap, 0.33, if you have reached potential cap.
7. Trainers (Trainer, Youth trainer) - (295510.13)
