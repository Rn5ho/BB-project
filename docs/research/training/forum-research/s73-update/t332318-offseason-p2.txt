More interesting answers from Marin:
DMI formula also changed? I wonder since we have a DMI to GS formula that fits GS decimals 
Yes, since DMI is based on a hidden attribute which has changed. We will try to set it to a similar level as before. 
Leaving things as they are for too long led to the situation where there were obvious loopholes and the whole game was shifting to some very specific builds and strategies. It is always better to try and fix things, even if it means breaking some eggs in the process. The BB community does not like change, this is well known, but in this regard, change was needed and, in my opinion, too late even. I will give my best to explain the changes and the reasoning behind them in the Q&A, as well as what can be expected for the future. 
Are you sure you're not actually boosting high IS guards and 3-2 strat performance?
If high ID guards are more expensive and cap faster, high IS guards will abuse this system even more won't they? 
High IS guards are also more expensive, so the effect should balance out. 
is there any skill that had a decrese?because almost everyone had huge increses in their salaries 
Most skills did. They are more spread out so the effects are not as visible. 
This is a new formula but it was engineered to follow the old one in most regards. It is also intended to fix loopholes, as possible while adhering to the first principle. Most of the salary increases you are seeing are a result of training, though, with some builds becoming more expensive by design. Other builds have become cheaper, in order to offset that so the total salary mass is slightly less than what it would be.
