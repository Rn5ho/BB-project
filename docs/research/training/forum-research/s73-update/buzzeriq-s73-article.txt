BuzzerBeater salary formula change (Season 73): what actually moved
By Ubiond
 ·
All news
Around 
4-5 August 2026
 (Season 72→73 rollover), BuzzerBeater shipped a new 
player salary formula
. As announced on Discord (BB-Marin and others), the pitch was: a simpler formula, close loopholes (cheap high 
ID
 on the perimeter), keep overall salary mass in a similar range, and tweak soft cap / DMI in the same pass.
This is a manager-facing summary of what seems to have moved. The
% figures below are estimates from our BuzzerIQ models
, fitted on large live player samples (pre vs post behaviour). Not official BB coefficients. Use them as a compass, then check your roster in the
Salary & Cap Estimator
.
Legend
Positions:
 PG point guard · SG shooting guard · SF small forward · PF power forward · C center
Skills:
 JS jump shot · JR jump range · OD outside defense · HA handling · DR driving · PA passing · IS inside shot · ID inside defense · RB rebounding · SB (BL) shot blocking
TL;DR
Guards / SF (perimeter):
ID
 is clearly more expensive.
IS
 a bit more too. Both still weigh less than PA / JS / OD.
JR
 and
PA
 a touch cheaper, especially PA on pure point guards and JR on shooting guards. Small shifts.
Bigs:
 classic IS/ID/RB with low
SB
 slightly cheaper.
SB (block)
 weighs more.
Overall, estimated salaries are a bit lower (roughly 
~−4%
 median vs the old formula).

~0 → costs

high ID

Block matters more
Training +1 ID/IS on a normal short still looks like a light secondary. The big jump is mainly 
ID
 on builds that are
already high
 (and IS a bit), not from one ordinary training season.
Soft cap / potential
Soft cap was adjusted with the salary change. Directionally you see the same themes in "how full" a player looks: perimeter
ID/IS
 count a bit more;
block
 a bit more for bigs; pure PA / JR a hair less. Official weights are not published.
We do 
not
 get a "this player is capped" flag from BBAPI, only skills + potential. To improve estimates, we need community help:
Call to action
If you have trainees you 
know
 are soft-capped (training clearly at 1/3), share them on the 
BuzzerIQ Discord
 for study. A short list of known capped players beats guessing from one snapshot.
DMI & form decimals
In BuzzerIQ we use 
DMI
 to estimate
game shape decimals
 (the hidden part of form between whole GS levels). Marin also confirmed DMI moved (tied to a changed hidden attribute), with an intent to keep levels similar. We are
following the evolution
 over the next weeks via roster snapshots (season-open GS≈7, later GS=9). More concrete numbers once the sample has matured. No formula swap on day one.
Personal note (meta)
On 
bigs
, I am fairly happy with the direction. If salary and cap both nudge toward more all-around interiors, and make pure "block-first, zero offense" less free, that helps break an old distortion: two stoppers parked as PGs because PA was nearly free on bigs, and IS-stuffed shorts parked as bigs. The vector is right. Fine-tuning will still come.
Same for cooling the eternal 
3-2 / SB-heavy
dominance: I like the intent. But
salaries alone are not enough
. You can still build expensive, SB-dominant rosters that win on the court. Rebalancing that meta almost certainly needs a hand on the 
match engine
, not only the wage bill.
On 
shorts
, I am having a harder time reading the changes. If the goal was to cool the stretch / 3-2 meta, taxing
ID on shorts
 is odd given that man-to-man defense is essential, while 
IS
 as a salary component barely moved and stays economically a secondary. We will probably keep seeing high-IS shorts. We will understand more in the coming days as a community, also after developer statements.
