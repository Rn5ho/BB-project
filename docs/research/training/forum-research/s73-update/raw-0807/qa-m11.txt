 Hey Marin, appreciate your work on this and dealing with a rowdy player base. I have a handful of questions

1. Salary and Training Cap
If in the old world, a player generally hit their cap around $X salary, does that hold true under the new formula? If yes, does that mean that today's elite SB bigs and IS guards will be fundamentally impossible to create in the future because they would cap before they hit those builds due to the higher salary cost of those skills?

2. D1 salary change vs D4
It seems that overall, salaries stayed mostly flat but that hides a trend that D1 teams/players mostly saw increases while lower divisions mostly saw decreases. Was this intentional and what was your goal here? How do you expect this to affect D1 team finances and team planning?

3. GE vs Salary changes
Do you have a framework for deciding when a problem should be fixed by a salary formula update vs changing the GE? Theoretically, you could "solve" the SB big problem by making it less effective in the GE. Curious what you can share on this and what it means moving forward

4. Future plans
I know plans change, but what can you share about your expectations for future salary and GE updates? Do you anticipate penalizing IS and SB more in the future? Do you anticipate major GE updates in the next ~3 seasons?

5. Training Rules
I expect you will share more info in the season update post, but what details can you share about how training will change other than elastics will become less impactful and SB will train some IS? Do you expect more training updates in the near future? If yes, what types of loopholes/incentives are you targeting?
