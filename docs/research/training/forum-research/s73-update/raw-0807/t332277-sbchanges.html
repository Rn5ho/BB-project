BB-Marin has shared some updates on Discord about the upcoming changes to the salary formula, especially the ones related to SB.

Although I'd recommend anyone who wants to stay as up to date as possible to join the Discord channel, I also understand that users who don't want to use Discord still deserve to have access to this information. So here's a summary of what has been said.

These are BB-Marin's exact words:

As I said before, the changes are coming, SB will be more expensive but player salaries won't jump more than 25% from what the old salaries would have been. We are still running tests and trying to give you the best possible improvements. It takes time and there is no way to disclose the process.

I will try to be open and transparent as much as I can when the changes are applied, and there is one thing I can say right away - there will be changes to the GE too, but mostly bug fixes and small adjustments, none of which will touch the SB effects on the GE, as SB changes will be limited to the new salary formula.

We are trying to be gentle with the changes while filling loopholes accumulated over many seasons, it is a challenge.

After Alonso asked: Is SB training going to include IS training or this is, as of now, GE + Salary Formula updates?

This was the answer: I am considering this still. I'm leaning towards yes for now.

If there is something new, I will try to bring it here. Other users who have a Discord account, feel free to share the news here as well.

New answer from Marin: The idea is to move towards a better salary formula which will cover all loopholes but this is a process, not a one time change.
