 Season 73: The New Salary Formula (part 1)
 

 
 
 
 The new salary formula, measured against what the old one would have charged for exactly the same players, actually lowered total salaries by about 2%. Around 85% of teams pay less than the old formula would have asked. If your roster costs more than last season, that is your players getting better via training or your team is basically choc full of monsters.
 
The old formula had accumulated thirty seasons of patches, became incredibly complex and impossible to tune. It also had one real problem: some skills were very cheap in several positions, allowing for some strange builds and rewarding weird lineups. The most glaring cases were shot blocking on all positions and inside scoring on guards.
 
Players with shot blocking are now priced accordingly. Centers as a group are slightly more expensive than the old formula would have made them, while other positions went down in pricing. In general, teams with no shot blocking specialists pay less, teams built around them pay more, roughly in proportion to how much they did it.
 
You might notice another thing: super high salary players (over 180k) got more expensive to keep, while the rest got cheaper. This is not a mistake, as this will level the playing field between top league teams with lots of additional points of revenue, compared to what lower division teams have access to.
 
We got some of it wrong the first time. The initial update made inside shot cheaper for guards and small forwards, which is the exact opposite of the intention, and shot blocking was not made expensive enough for them either. Our community detected the issue within 24 hours, and a second update the next evening fixed both. Inside shot for guards and small forwards now sits slightly above where the old formula had it. Thanks to everyone who dug into the numbers!
 
There is a full Q&A thread in the forums with much more detail. 
 
Training changes
 
The elastic effect — where a high level in one skill speeds up the training of a related one — has been reduced. The effect has been trimmed very slightly across the board. Expect skill progression to be a touch slower overall.
 
Two training types were rebalanced. Shot Blocking now also trains inside shot, with its inside defense and rebounding contributions reduced. Inside Scoring trains more inside defense and slightly less jump shot. Both changes tighten these regimens around the inside game rather than spreading them thin.

 Season 73: The New Salary Formula (part 2)
 

 
 
 
 Game engine changes
 
We found and fixed several small bugs and inconsistencies. Their effects are small, but will help reduce some annoyances. Read on for some specifics on the most important ones.
 
Rebounding now depends on energy. A tired player rebounds worse than a fresh one, which was not the case before. The effect is modest for now, but it does mean that riding the starters will cost you on the boards late in games, which incentivizes longer benches. Expect this effect to grow in future seasons.
 
Rebounds knocked out of bounds are now much less frequent and depend on position — a guard tipping the ball out is now far rarer than a center doing it, where before every player did it at the same rate. And when an offensive player does knock it out, it now correctly counts as a turnover. It never did before, so expect team and player turnover numbers to be slightly higher than you are used to.
 
Finally, a long standing bug where experience was applied repeatedly in overtime has been fixed. Games going to multiple overtimes were giving players a substantial and entirely unintended boost. 

We will continue to try and improve the GE next season, with one, not so unexpected, change: 3-2 defense will be weakened!
 
Coming next season: stricter hoarding tax
 
The hoarding tax will be increased from 10% to 20%, and the threshold at which it starts to apply will be lowered from 25 million to 15 million. This is a season's notice, so plan accordingly, with future expansions on the tax also possible.

 Season 71: the Utopia Expansion and Skyscraper League Structure
 

 
 
 
 Utopia has always performed better than anyone expected, hence the improvised expansion of its 4th division, from the usual 64 leagues to the very unwieldy 96 it ended up with. We tried to give all Utopia managers the same starting circumstances, but in the process, promotion and demotion dynamics suffered (only one team could progress to division 3 per league). Once we almost filled all 96 div 4 leagues, the only solution to prevent running out of room was a reorganization, as we announced last season. 

Yes, Utopia now has the usual 64 division 4 leagues, and yes, we had to demote a large number of teams to division 5, BUT, we have also limited the number of division 5 leagues to 64, which makes promotion much easier (6 teams per league!) AND we are making sure the division 5 teams have the same financial circumstances as their division 4 counterparts. Lower divisions of Utopia, should they be necessary, will also adhere to these rules (64 leagues, same circumstances for all teams). 

Furthermore, inspired by the new “Skyscraper” shaped league organisation of Utopia, we’ve decided to implement the same to all countries with more than 4 divisions! Up to now, each 5th division used to consist of 256 leagues, making the league organization sort of like a pyramid, but now, they also have just 64 leagues. This will streamline many back-end processes while leaving lots of room for new signups.

Enough about the leagues, we still have to address the Garbage Time implementation! Since last season, we’ve added the option of disabling garbage time from triggering in private leagues, which passed the test, and from this season on, the same option is available in scrimmages! If the option passes muster, we will implement it for all match types next season. 

On the other hand, we are working on some other adjustments affecting training, stamina, the sports psychologist and related aspects of the game to promote better minute management and discourage short rosters. More details on that will be announced in a separate news post when the changes are finalized.

 Welcome to Season 64: AutoBid!
 

 
 
 
 Finally, the much requested AutoBid feature is here! Bidding on both players and staff has just become much easier. The way it works is very similar to auction sites; instead of bidding a fixed amount (which you can still do - we didn’t remove the old Bid button), you can now set an amount (maximum bid) up to which you are ready to bid. The system will then bid for you just the lowest necessary amount to take over the auction (a difference of at least $1000 or 2% still applies). 

Other bidders can then make bids (normal or automatic ones) to try and overbid you, but once the max bid has been overbid, even by $1, that’s it, the new bidder has taken over! In case of ties, the priority is with the earlier bid, “automatic” or “normal”. But, in the end, the highest bid wins, just as it always has.

The system checks the available amount of cash of the max bid holder with each bid, so not having enough funds to cover it will result in an overbid.  Team consolidation: addendum and clarification
 

 
 
 
 Our plan to consolidate all leagues is going forward, but since there were some concerns and ambiguity, we will try to clarify the process, as much as we can.

As an example, we should take a country with semi-filled division III. The top league will, of course, stay as it is. Division II also, as it will be filled completely by the newly promoted human controlled teams. Division III will be consolidated as follows: human teams go to leagues with lower numbers (III.1, III.2, etc...), while "fresh" teams go to highest available numbers (III.16, III.15, etc...) and bots fill the middle. There should be, if available, at least 4 bots per league in all cases to make sure there are at least a couple of easy wins for everyone. 

In leagues where there's too many human controlled teams to make the consolidation work as described, placing "fresh" teams separately with 4 bots will take preference over placement of other human controlled teams. 

We expect to have special cases and some possible niggles in some countries, but we will follow common sense and try to fix the placements as best we can, making changes as we go. Exciting times ahead!
