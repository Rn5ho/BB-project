18 yo- The first pop is irrelevant because we don't know sublevels. although both popped 4 times in 8 weeks, the younger popped the first week, indicated preexisting sublevel. additionally the 18yo went 3 weeks before a pop
The 21 yo popped every 2 weeks like clockwork.
The 23 yo popping 3 times in 8 weeks at 46 minutes is interesting, however 3 week gap between 4 and 7.
The 21 y/o definitely trained at a pop every 2 weeks or faster.
The 18&23yo were training slower, closer to 3 weeks a pop than 2weeks most likely for the 23 y/o. I suspect teh 18 y/o was somewhere in the middle.
