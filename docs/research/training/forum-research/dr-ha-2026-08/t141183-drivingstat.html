"Driving: Allows the player to create his own shot without a good pass from a teammate. Better driving creates shots more often and creates better shots on average."



Three questions about Driving. When I think of driving, I think of a player putting the ball on the floor and taking it to the hole.



1) Has anyone noticed on their roster, if their players with good driving draw more fouls and get to the free throw line more often? Or no relation?


2) Can you skimp on driving if you have good passing? If you have a good passing team that generates alot of assists, what is really the purpose of having this skill as it is defined in the game manual?
Do you all recommend for it to be "ok" to skimp in driving stat if you have a good passing team, provided driving does not increase fouls and free throw attempts?

3) When the game says that Driving "creates more shots", does this mean you shoot earlier in the shot clock as well thus resulting in more possesions or something?
